in the bounded C++ host:

| Capability | C++ declaration | Portable observation |
| --- | --- | --- |

scheduler/fairness guarantees, thread identity/count, timing thresholds, and

registry, shared-spec runner, and evidence format. The private
`r25_concurrency` fixture adds only deterministic accepted/completed quiescence
for causal observation; it is unavailable to ordinary programs.

The six R25 shared cases cover:

- exact Ref observations and a causal blocking Process producer;
- Cell FIFO success/failure, restart generation isolation, and nested-send
